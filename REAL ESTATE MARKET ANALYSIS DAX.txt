REAL ESTATE MARKET ANALYSIS
DAX MEASURES
===========================

1. Total Sales

Total Sales =
SUM(PropertySales[SalesAmount])


2. Total Properties Sold

Total Properties Sold =
COUNTROWS(PropertySales)


3. Average Property Price

Average Property Price =
AVERAGE(PropertySales[SalesAmount])


4. Total Property Area

Total Property Area =
SUM(PropertySales[PropertyAreaSqFt])


5. Average Price per Sq Ft

Average Price per Sq Ft =
DIVIDE(
    [Total Sales],
    [Total Property Area],
    0
)
